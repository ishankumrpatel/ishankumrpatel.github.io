About

Curious. Practical. People-first.

I believe good health and personal growth should be simple, shared and sustainable. That belief is why I started Rise & Thrive: a place where people learn together, support each other and see real change.

I turn complicated topics into clear, usable steps, and I build communities where members feel they belong. My earlier career in technology and operations shaped how I think about systems and consistency, and I bring that into everything I build.

Based in Copenhagen, I work in English, Danish, Gujarati and Hindi.
